## What this shows, and what it cannot

- An unintended wire (right amount, right payee, right rule, no confirmation) is refused, and so is every stand-in: the classifier's key, another customer's key, a stale confirmation, a confirmation for a different request, a payer not in the registry. Only the payer's own key, over this request, issues the ticket.
- This is opt-in: the reference rulebook is unchanged, so a deployment turns it on for the accounts and rules that warrant it.
- What it cannot cover, as the thread conceded: a customer who confirms a mistake, or who is talked into confirming. That stays a person, the door `docs/23` concedes. The chip ensures the person was asked and that the agent could not answer for them; it cannot ensure the person was right.

# Contributing to the discussion

- Post in [Discussions](../../discussions), in Q&A or Ideas. Please don't open pull requests against the product
  repositories with design text; propose it here instead.
- You keep the copyright in what you write. By posting you share it under
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): anyone may quote and build on it with credit to you.
- Be specific: name the claim, the file or the experiment you are questioning, and what would change your mind.
- Criticism is welcome. The project publishes its failures next to its results; a good objection gets the same
  treatment.

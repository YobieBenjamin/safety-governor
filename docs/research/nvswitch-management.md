# Evidence register: NVSwitch and NVLink management authority (October 2026)

An independent researcher's register of what a governor on the management card can do to NVLink traffic: Fabric Manager's partition APIs, the open NVSwitch driver's controls, the out-of-band surfaces (SMBPBI, NSM, the HMC's Redfish), the NVL72 switch trays, partition binding, confidential-computing modes, and the hardware straps. It ends with what is available today with no NVIDIA cooperation, what Fabric Manager honouring a governor-signed policy adds, and seven one-line firmware asks. Each row carries its evidence level and source.


Scope: what a safety governor on the rack management card can do to NVLink traffic on HGX (NVSwitch gen3 "LS10"/H100, gen4 B200) and GB200/GB300 NVL72. Primary sources only: NVIDIA docs, NVIDIA open source (open-gpu-kernel-modules 615.78.08 @ca2d03e, nsmd @b6cddce, bmcweb @c29d780, phosphor-dbus-interfaces), OCP, NVIDIA forum staff replies. Limit: docs.nvidia.com pages/PDFs truncate at ~100 k chars in the fetch tool, so FM guide ch. 9 to 13 (HA modes, exclusion bodies) were unreadable; those items are PARTIAL.

Legend: **V** VERIFIED (URL + quote) · **P** PARTIAL · **U** UNKNOWN. FM guide = https://docs.nvidia.com/datacenter/tesla/fabric-manager-user-guide/index.html ; OGKM = https://github.com/NVIDIA/open-gpu-kernel-modules.

## 2. Open-source NVSwitch driver (PCIe, in-band) , all V

Files: OGKM `src/common/nvswitch/interface/ctrl_dev_nvswitch.h`, `ioctl_common_nvswitch.h`, `kernel/nvswitch.c`, `kernel-open/nvidia/linux_nvswitch.c`.

- Routing/containment per port: `CTRL_NVSWITCH_SET_SWITCH_PORT_CONFIG` (0x02; `CONNECT_ACCESS_GPU|ACCESS_CPU|TRUNK_SWITCH|ACCESS_SWITCH`, `requesterLinkID`, `trunkSrcMask`), `SET_REMAP_POLICY`/`_VALID` (0x0D/0x1D), `SET_ROUTING_ID`/`_VALID` (0x0E/0x1A), `SET_ROUTING_LAN`/`_VALID` (0x0F/0x16), `SET_MC_RID_TABLE` (0x28). Remap doc: "Packets that fail compare are converted to UR response and looped back." Hardware rejects surface as `NVSWITCH_ERR_HW_NPORT_INGRESS_ACLFAIL`, `_REQCONTEXTMISMATCHERR`, `_ADDRBOUNDSERR`, `_ADDRTYPEERR`, `_RIDTABCFGERR`, `NPORT_ROUTE_NOPORTDEFINEDERR`.
- Link control: `CTRL_NVSWITCH_UNREGISTER_LINK` (0x13) "ensures that the black-listed link will not be initialized or trained by the driver."; `RESET_AND_DRAIN_LINKS` (0x14) "Resets NVLinks and ensures to drain backed up traffic."; `SET_CONTINUOUS_ALI`/`REQUEST_ALI` (0x63/0x64).
- Device/fabric state: `BLACKLIST_DEVICE` (0x20) "A blacklisted device will have interrupts disabled, and opens/ioctls will fail. If a device is blacklisted OOB then the setting is persistent."; `SET_FM_DRIVER_STATE` (0x21), `SET_DEVICE_FABRIC_STATE` (0x22), `SET_FM_HEARTBEAT_TIMEOUT` (0x23, default 10 s → `MANAGER_TIMEOUT`); enums `DRIVER_FABRIC_STATE_{OFFLINE,STANDBY,CONFIGURED,MANAGER_TIMEOUT,MANAGER_ERROR}`, `DEVICE_FABRIC_STATE_{…,BLACKLISTED}`, reasons `MANUAL_OUT_OF_BAND`, `MANUAL_IN_BAND`, `TRUNK/ACCESS_LINK_FAILURE`. No "degraded" ioctl , degradation is FM policy. Raw: `REGISTER_READ/WRITE` (0x4D/0x4E), `I2C_SMBUS_COMMAND` (0x2C); FM↔GPU inband channel `INBAND_SEND_DATA/READ_DATA` (0x43/0x44).
- Privilege: `_nvswitch_lib_validate_privileged_ctrl` = superuser (`NV_IS_SUSER()`) **or** an fd on `/proc/driver/nvidia-nvlink/capabilities/fabric-mgmt` via `ACQUIRE_CAPABILITY` (0x18). So host root with PCIe access can rewrite routing regardless of FM.
- OOB hooks: at init the driver reads `NV_NVLSAW_SCRATCH_COLD_OOB_BLACKLIST_DEVICE_REQUESTED` (set by SOE on an OOB request) → `BLACKLISTED`/`MANUAL_OUT_OF_BAND` (`lr10.c: nvswitch_read_oob_blacklist_state_lr10`, reused by LS10); it publishes state to `NV_NVLSAW_SW_SCRATCH_12` ("used to communicate fabric state to SOE": `DEVICE_BLACKLIST_REASON`, `DEVICE_FABRIC_STATE`, `DRIVER_FABRIC_STATE`, `FABRIC_MANAGER_ERROR`).
- Firmware-disabled links cannot be revived by the host: `link_ls10.c: nvswitch_load_link_disable_settings_ls10` , if `NVLIPT_LNK_CTRL_LINK_STATE_STATUS_CURRENTLINKSTATE == _DISABLE` → `valid = NV_FALSE`, `nvlink_lib_unregister_link`, `nvswitch_destroy_link`. VBIOS `nvlink_config.link_disable_mask` also masks links (`rom_nvswitch.c`).
- TNVL (PPCIe): `SET_DEVICE_TNVL_LOCK` (0x69) "FM sets the TNVL lock after Fabric State is CONFIGURED"; `GET_TNVL_STATUS`, `GET_ATTESTATION_REPORT`. Writes route via SOE and are refused once locked (`tnvl_ls10.c`: "TNVL mode is locked").
- GPU side: `RMNvLinkDisableLinks`/`2` "The supplied value is ANDed with the set of discovered … links to remove unwanted links. A value of DISABLE_ALL removes/disables all links on this device." (`nvrm_registry.h`); `NV2080_CTRL_CMD_NVLINK_PROCESS_INIT_DISABLED_LINKS` "Process the HW-detected init disabled NVLinks and filter those out from discovered links." (`ctrl2080nvlink.h`).

## 5. Partition binding and unpartitioned GPUs

- **Binding (HGX):** after reset the GPU sends an in-band `GPU_PROBE_REQ` via the switch; FM replies with `fabricPartitionId`, `cliqueId`, GPA/FLA ranges, `linkMaskToBeReduced`, `epoch` ("Epoch to be matched by RM when allowing P2P between GPUs"), `fabricHealthMask` incl. `PARTITION_ASSIGNED_FALSE`, `INCORRECT_CONFIGURATION_NO_PARTITION` (**V**, OGKM `src/common/nvlink/inband/interface/nvlink_inband_msg.h`). Driver refuses P2P on clique mismatch: "GPU %d and Peer GPU %d cliqueId doesn't match" (**V**, `kernel_nvlink.c`). NVML: `nvmlGpuFabricInfo_t{clusterUuid, cliqueId, state}` (**V**, nvml.h in https://github.com/NVIDIA/go-nvml).
- **Enforcement is in the switch:** "For security, NVSwitch's routing tables are indexed and controlled by the NVIDIA fabric manager, providing protection by limiting an application's access to its specific ranges." (**V**, https://images.nvidia.com/content/pdf/nvswitch-technical-overview.pdf) plus §2 ACL/REQCTXT rejects. Shared-NVSwitch guests cannot see the fabric: "the fabrics are not visible to guests."; H100+ GPUs "communicate over NVLink by transmitting specialized packets to the FM that runs on the service VM." (**V**)
- **Root without FM:** bare metal/full passthrough , yes, switch BAR0 is host-visible and ioctls need only root (§2); the guide puts it on the hypervisor: "The hypervisor needs to maintain the partition configuration, including which NVLink connections to block on each GPU and switch for each partition." / "implement MMIO filtering for NVSwitch." (**V**). Shared-NVSwitch/vGPU , no: switches "are always assigned as a PCIe passthrough device to the service VM" (**V**). TNVL-locked , no (§2). NVL72 , no PCIe path; PKEY/routing are NVLSM's (**V**); GPU-side PKEY programming **U** (no `pkey` code in the open driver).
- **GPU in no partition:** FM drops its probe: "received NVLink inband message arrived on an NVLink port NodeId 0 NVSwitch 1 port 47 which is not part of any active partition"; NVIDIA staff: "indicates that the GPU partition may not be activated" (**V**, forums.developer.nvidia.com/t/…/310242); fabric state never COMPLETED, CUDA 802 "System not yet initialized" (reporter, **P**). NVL72: "newly discovered GPUs are not allocated to the Default Partition and cannot be used to run a workload until they are allocated to a partition."; with Default Partition off "the NVLink domain is configured to not have any GPUs communicate with each other at boot time." (**V**, Partition Guide).

## 6. Confidential computing and NVLink

- Single-GPU CC (Hopper): "Only one GPU per CVM is allowed." / "operations that involve multiple GPUs, such as peer-to-peer communications, are not supported." (**V**, RN-12523-001_r580 https://docs.nvidia.com/580trd1-trusted-computing-solutions-release-notes.pdf). Physical NVLink-off in CC-On: **U** (GSP-side; settle with `nvidia-smi -q` on a CC-On GPU or the Hopper CC whitepaper).
- PPCIe (HGX 8-way H100/H200): "multiple Hopper GPUs interconnected by NVSwitch or NVLink can be passed through to one CVM." / **"GPU-GPU communications over the NVLink or NVSwitch interconnect are not encrypted."** / "P2P communication is only supported over NVLINK." (**V**, same). Switches are attested: attestation changed "to measure both GPUs and switches configuration running in PPCIE mode" (**V**, https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/announcing-nvidia-secure-ai-general-availability). Mode is in-band and persistent: "can be toggled into and out of CC modes only with a privileged call from in the host"; "persistent across reboots and power cycles" (**V**, DU-12302-001_v7.1 https://docs.nvidia.com/cc-deployment-guide-tdx.pdf) , yet NSM defines OOB `NSM_SET_CONFIDENTIAL_COMPUTE_MODE_V1` and permission `RP_CONFIDENTIAL_COMPUTE` (**V**, nsmd).
- Blackwell: `RmCCMultiGpuNvle` ("Enable CC multi gpu mode with NVLE"), `RmNvlinkEncryptionWithCC`, AES-256-GCM NVLE keys under SPDM (**V**, `nvrm_registry.h`, `conf_compute.c`); no public GA statement for NVLE+NVSwitch CC found (**P**).
- Governor relevance: in PPCIe/TNVL the switch routing is locked after FM programs it and is attested, so a hostile host cannot re-route; CC never *stops* NVLink traffic, it isolates (Hopper) or encrypts (Blackwell) inside the attested set.

## 7. Hardware NVLink-disable pin/strap

- OCP HGX spec lists only `PEX_STRAP[1:0]` ("PCIe strapping for reference clock and reset configuration"), `PEX_RST0..2_N`, USB, I2C, unnamed GPIO , no NVLink/NVSwitch disable (**V** absence).
- NVSwitch driver GPIO functions: `THERMAL_EVENT, OVERTEMP, THERMAL_ALERT, THERMAL_CRITICAL, POWER_ALERT, INSTANCE_ID0..9` only (**V**, `rom_nvswitch.h`). Link enable comes from VBIOS mask and firmware link state.
- Verdict **U**. **Settles:** NVIDIA HGX baseboard pinout/CPLD spec (NDA) or the NVL72 switch-tray schematic.

## (a) Authority today, no NVIDIA cooperation

1. **Whole-device off, persistent:** SMBPBI/NSM device-disable (OOB blacklist) of any GPU or NVSwitch; driver honours it at next load ("If a device is blacklisted OOB then the setting is persistent."). Plus HMC `Switch.Reset` and chassis/GPU power actions. Coarse (kills the device, not just NVLink) and effective at reset, not instantly.
2. **Observe:** FM state, device-disable state and NVLink link state via SMBPBI; NSM `GET_FABRIC_MANAGER_STATE`; 10 s FM heartbeat → `MANAGER_TIMEOUT`. Cannot read routing tables OOB.
3. **If the platform HMC build enables them** (code public, enablement per-platform): NSM `PortDisableFuture` on chosen GPU/NVSwitch ports (from next reset; host driver then cannot see or train them), `SwitchIsolationMode`, and `InbandReconfigPermissions.NVLinkDisable` = disallow host , the one lever that makes a policy non-revocable by root.
4. **NVL72:** per-port `link state down` and partition add/remove only through NVOS REST/NMX-C, i.e., the governor must hold switch-tray admin or mTLS credentials; the Switch BMC alone can only power-cycle the tray.
5. **Not available:** instantaneous OOB per-link kill; OOB read/pin of routing tables; any protection on bare-metal/full-passthrough hosts where root reprograms the switch over PCIe.

## (b) With FM honouring a governor-signed partition policy (software only)

Run FM in Shared-NVSwitch mode (`FABRIC_MODE=1`) in a governor-trusted Service VM (NVLSM/NMX-C on NVL72); the policy is the allowed partition set; the host gets NVLink P2P only through `fmActivateFabricPartition` of a signed partition ID. FM then enforces in hardware: "Programming NVSwitch to deny access to GPUs not assigned to the partition." / "Disable the corresponding NVSwitch routing and GPU access." GPUs outside an active partition get no probe response and cannot initialise CUDA. Add `FM_CMD_UNIX_SOCKET_PATH` (no TCP), governor-controlled state-file replay, `MANAGER_TIMEOUT` watch, and OOB `PortDisableFuture`/device-disable as fallback when FM is not CONFIGURED. Residual gaps: FM socket has no authentication; Service-VM/host root stays in the TCB (only PPCIe/TNVL lock + attestation on H100/H200 makes routing immutable); denial takes effect at the next (de)activation or reset, never instantly.

## (c) Firmware asks (one line each)

1. Document and guarantee `NSM_SET_PORT_DISABLE_FUTURE` / `NSM_SET_SWITCH_ISOLATION_MODE` on HGX H100/B200 NVSwitch SOE and GB200 GPU firmware, with an "apply now (reset-and-drain listed links)" variant, not only "from next reset".
2. Ship HMC Redfish `Switches/*/Ports/*/Settings` (PortDisableFuture) and `Oem.Nvidia.SwitchIsolationMode` enabled by default, with an ERoT-enforced write permission limited to the BMC-side governor identity.
3. Make `InbandReconfigPermissions.NVLinkDisable` (and `CCMode`, `BAR0Firewall`) OOB-lockable so `Allow*Config=false` cannot be cleared by any in-band or DOE path until AC cycle.
4. Add an SMBPBI/NSM "fabric policy hash" computed by SOE over the live remap/RID/RLAN tables so a governor can attest routing OOB instead of trusting FM.
5. Add FM option `FM_PARTITION_POLICY_PUBKEY` requiring `fmActivateFabricPartition` (and NMX-C `AddGpusToPartition`) requests to carry a signature over `{partitionId, GPU UUIDs, epoch}` verified before routing is programmed.
6. Expose on the NVL72 Switch BMC a Redfish `Oem/Nvidia` action forcing NVSwitch ASIC port disable independent of NVOS credentials.
7. Publish the NSM spec (Types 1, 5) and the HGX HMC Redfish OEM schema so the above can be verified without NDA.

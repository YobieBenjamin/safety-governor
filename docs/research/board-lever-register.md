# Evidence register: lever signals per board (October 2026)

An independent researcher's register of what the public record says about the three per-slot levers, SMBPBI and the baseboard interfaces, with an evidence level per row (VERIFIED with a quoted primary source, PARTIAL, UNKNOWN) and the vendor documents that would settle the unknowns. It feeds the board matrix the design will carry; until then it is the primary record. Sources are the URLs cited in each row.


Levers per GPU slot: (1) hardware power brake, (2) per-slot PERST#, (3) per-slot power enable; plus (4) SMBPBI reach, (5) PCIe-switch / HGX-connector management. Levels: **VERIFIED** = primary source quoted; **PARTIAL** = primary source implies it, or only a secondary source states it; **UNKNOWN** = nothing public.

## Summary

On **PCIe-card servers** the power brake is real and documented at the platform level only by Lenovo (BIOS "PCIe Power Brake", PSU-alert or budget driven, any >=75 W card); NVIDIA confirms the GPU-side input via NVML, and OCP OAM/NIC specs confirm it is the CEM "Power Brake" pin (B30 per a secondary source) with a pull-up on the card, so a management card that can reach the slot's B30 net (directly or through the PCIe switch/riser CPLD) has lever (1) with no new GPU silicon; whether it is one shared net or per slot is OEM-specific and undocumented. Per-slot PERST# is a standard CEM pin and PCIe switches (PEX89000 "hot-plug via I2C", Switchtec per-port hot-plug controllers over TWI/UART) give an out-of-band path to lever (2), but no OEM documents per-slot power enable (3): slots are "not hot-swappable" (Dell), so (3) exists only where the riser has an e-fuse/hot-swap EN the BMC can reach. On **HGX H100/H200/B200** the host connector carries 2x I2C, PCIe x1 to the FPGA, USB to the HMC and 3.3 V GPIO; reset is grouped (3 PERST inputs, default 1), a single board-level power-brake state is visible to the BMC, SMBPBI power cap is reachable and persistent, and per-GPU power/reset are inside the FPGA/HMC and undocumented. On **GB200 NVL72** the BMC itself owns `PWR_BRAKE_L`, `RUN_POWER_EN` and `SYS_RST_IN_L` as tray-level lines, with chassis-level `CHASSIS_PWR_BRK_L` and `TRAY_FAST_SHDN_L` inputs; per-GPU levers are not public. **DC-SCM** gives the governor card enough I2C (18), GPIO-over-LTPI (16+16 default) and UART reach, but explicitly excludes per-endpoint PERST, so slot levers must be wired via HPM CPLD/FPGA GPIO or the PCIe switch. **OCP OAI/UBB** is the only public baseboard family with all three levers per module (per-OAM hot-swap + e-fuse, per-OAM PERST, HOST_PWRGD, PWRBRK#).
